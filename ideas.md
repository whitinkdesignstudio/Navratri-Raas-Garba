## Chosen Approach: Rangon Ki Raat

### Design Movement
Contemporary Indian editorial design with tactile poster-making references: high-contrast display typography, generous cream space, expressive brush marks, and a human-first narrative arc.

### Core Principles
1. **Celebrate, then invite contribution:** every emotional beat leads naturally toward donation, sponsorship, or tickets.
2. **Tactile warmth:** cream paper, ink edges, brush textures, and imperfect circular motifs echo the supplied poster.
3. **Premium restraint:** use orange and magenta as purposeful accents against a quiet base; avoid visual clutter and heavy black.
4. **Mobile-first clarity:** thumb-friendly CTAs and a sticky action rail make the three conversion paths obvious.

### Color Philosophy
The base is a soft cream inspired by the poster paper and chosen to feel welcoming and optimistic. Saffron orange carries celebration and movement; deep magenta carries generosity and emotional urgency; burgundy provides readable contrast and trust. The ownable signature color is **Raas Magenta #B91F59**, used for primary giving actions and the most meaningful highlights.

### Layout Paradigm
A left-anchored editorial composition with staggered content blocks, a split hero, diagonal brush accents, and a donation control that breaks the page rhythm. Content should feel arranged like a campaign poster wall rather than a centered SaaS template.

### Signature Elements
- Hand-drawn sunburst and radial dots as small navigational motifs.
- Rough brush underlines behind key phrases and CTA labels.
- A thin burgundy editorial rule with tiny orange/magenta diamond markers.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like turning a page or choosing a meaningful contribution: gentle lift, ink-darkening, and clear selected states. Donation choices update the supporting microcopy immediately; all CTAs have a visible purpose and anchor to the relevant section.

### Animation
Use short, quiet reveals for content groups, a slow floating drift for the radial sun motif, and 160–220ms transitions for cards and buttons. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Avoid continuous decorative motion that competes with the cause.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for emotional headlines and **Manrope** for body copy, labels, and controls. Headlines use sentence case or intentional uppercase sparingly; small labels use compact letter spacing. Keep body copy between 16–19px with strong line height.

### Brand Essence
A premium Navratri celebration for Indian families and community members who want their night of Garba to create meaningful support for children in India. Personality: **warm, expressive, purposeful**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines feel emotionally direct and culturally familiar; CTAs are active and human, never corporate. Microcopy reassures people that every amount matters without unsupported impact claims.

Example lines:
- “Come for the Garba. Leave a little more hope behind.”
- “Choose the way you want your celebration to count.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact circular sun-and-dandiya mark: a hand-drawn orange sun behind two crossed magenta dandiya sticks, paired with a narrow uppercase wordmark for “NAVRATRI RAAS GARBA”. The mark should work as a standalone favicon and header icon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Raas Magenta — #B91F59**

### Style Decisions
- Theme colors are sampled from the supplied poster: warm cream, saffron orange, magenta, and burgundy.
- The supplied poster is the hero visual reference; supporting visual assets use the same human, hopeful, brush-textured language.
- Donation, sponsorship, and ticket actions remain visible in the header, hero, and sticky mobile action bar.
